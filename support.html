Frequently Asked Questions

1. I can't sign in. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and make sure you are using the same email or Sign in with Apple method you used to create your account. If the problem continues, contact support from the email address on your account.

2. I didn't receive a verification code.

Check your spam or junk folder and confirm your email address is correct. You can request a new code after a short wait.

3. How do I pair with my partner?

After signing up, share your 6-character invite code from "Invite your person" (codes are valid for 24 hours). Your partner taps "Enter it", types the code, and confirms. You can be paired with one partner at a time.

4. Why can't I see my partner's frames yet?

That's the point of film. Until a roll is developed, your partner's frames stay latent: you only see how many there are and where they were taken. When the roll is full, or either of you taps "Finish roll", it develops for 24 hours and then every frame is revealed to both of you.

5. How do I manage permissions?

Memora asks for Photos, Location, Notifications, and App Tracking Transparency only when a feature needs them. You can change any choice in Us > Permissions or in iOS Settings > Memora.

6. How do I report or block someone?

To report a frame, open it and tap ⋯ > Report this frame. To report or block your partner, go to Us > Partner. Blocked people are listed in Us > Account > Blocked. We review every report within 24 hours.

7. How do I report a bug?

Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, a description of the issue, and screenshots if possible.

8. How do I delete my account?

Open Us > Account > Delete account and confirm. This removes your profile and the frames you shot, as described in our Privacy Policy. You can also email us to request deletion.

9. The app isn't working properly.

Try restarting the app, updating to the latest version, and restarting your iPhone. If the problem continues, please contact us.
