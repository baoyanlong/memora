2. Description of the Service

Memora is a private shared roll of film for two partners. Features include, but are not limited to:

  • Rolls: Start a roll for a trip, with a name, optional destination, dates, and 24 or 36 frames. A couple has one loaded roll at a time.
  • Shooting: Add photos from your library as frames, with an optional short caption and location. Until a roll is developed, your partner's frames stay hidden as latent images.
  • Darkroom: When a roll is full or finished, it develops for 24 hours before both partners can see every frame as a contact sheet, a frame-by-frame viewer, and a route map.
  • Map: See where your developed frames were taken.
  • Hearts and saving: Mark developed frames with a heart and save frames or contact sheets to your photo library.
  • Reminders: Optional notifications when a roll is developed, when your partner adds a frame, and for "On this day" memories.

We may update, modify, or improve features at any time.
