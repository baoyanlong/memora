Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Memora ("Memora," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and safeguard information when you use the Memora iOS app and related services. Memora is a private shared roll of film for exactly two linked partners.

Short version: We use your information to run your account and the rolls you share with your partner. We do not sell your personal information. There is no public feed, profile search, or chat with strangers. You control optional permissions such as Photos, Location, Notifications, and App Tracking Transparency.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile information

When you register or sign in, we collect your email address, a unique user identifier (UID), and the sign-in method you choose, such as email or Sign in with Apple. If Apple provides a private relay address, we use that address for your account. Your profile may include your name, birthday, gender, and an optional profile photo.

Pairing information

When you invite or connect with a partner, we process the one-time invite code, your partner connection status, and an optional anniversary date you choose to save.

Rolls and frames

When you use Memora, you may create rolls (with a name, optional destination, dates, and capacity) and add frames to them. A frame includes the photo you pick from your library, an optional short caption, the frame number, who shot it, and when it was added. You may also mark developed frames with a heart. Photos you choose are uploaded to our media storage so they can be synced with your partner.

Location information

To place frames on your shared map and roll route, Memora reads the location stored in a photo you pick, if there is one. If a picked photo has no location and you grant permission while using the app, Memora may use your device's current location once for that frame. You can clear the location of a frame before adding it. We do not track your location in the background and do not show your live location to your partner.

Safety reports and blocks

If you report a frame or a partner, we collect the reason you select, any optional details you add, and the reported content. If you block someone, we record that block so it can be enforced.

Device, usage, and diagnostic information

We receive information such as device model, iOS version, app version, language, time zone, a device or installation identifier, IP address, and request timestamps. Firebase Analytics may process events such as sign-in, registration, and feature interactions. We use this information to understand reliability and improve Memora.

Notifications and attribution

If you allow notifications, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging provide a device push token so we can tell you when a roll is developed, when your partner adds a frame, and about "On this day" memories. Adjust may process advertising and install attribution information. Memora asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before using data that requires that permission. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide sign-in, profile, pairing, rolls, frames, developing, hearts, maps, and reminders.
  • Sync rolls and frames between the two linked partners.
  • Place frames on the shared map and roll route.
  • Send the notifications you enable and maintain notification delivery.
  • Measure app performance, diagnose errors, understand feature use, and improve the service.
  • Review reports, enforce blocks, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate Memora:

  • Your linked partner: Rolls, frames, captions, frame locations, hearts, and your profile name and photo. Before a roll is developed, your partner sees only the number and place names of your frames, not the photos.
  • Service providers: Our account, hosting, and media storage providers, Apple services, Firebase (Cloud Messaging and Analytics), and Adjust process information on our behalf.
  • Safety and legal purposes: We may disclose information to investigate abuse, protect users, comply with law, or protect our rights.
  • Business changes: Information may transfer as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

4. Storage and Security

Some settings and cached data are stored on your device. Account data, rolls, and frames are processed on our servers, and photos are stored through our media infrastructure. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Retention, Unpairing, and Account Deletion

We keep information while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. If you unpair, neither partner can open the shared rolls anymore; pairing again starts a new, empty space.

You can delete your account in the app under Us > Account > Delete account. We then delete your profile and the frames you shot, subject to limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or backups that expire in the ordinary course. Frames your partner shot remain on your partner's side. Your partner may also keep copies of frames they saved to their own device.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You have the right to access your personal data, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your account and associated data, and withdraw consent for optional processing. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as receiving a copy of your data or objecting to certain processing. You can manage iOS permissions in Settings, use the in-app account controls, or contact us at support@memora.app. We may need to verify your request before completing it.

7. Children

Memora is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

8. International Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Continued use of Memora after an update means the updated Policy applies.
